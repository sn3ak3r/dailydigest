PREFER
- Security: new technique, exploit chain, or malware analysis with  actual detail. Windows internals and RE tooling rank highest.
- Gaming: technical or industry substance — engine work, interesting indie games,studio news, notable releases.
- Science: a result, not a press release about a result.
- Projects: something built, with the build documented.

DEMOTE
- Vendor marketing, funding rounds, "top 10 tips", awards.
- Breach announcements with no technical detail.
- Opinion//editorial with no new information.
- Anything already covered by another item in the same run.

DIGEST SLOTS (2)
Pick the two with the most technical depth, regardless of topic.
Prefer an arXiv paper or a research writeup over a news item.
