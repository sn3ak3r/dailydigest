## Deep Digests

### AmnesiaStealer: live remote control of your own browser

**Problem:** Stealing static passwords is easy to defend against; getting live access to a victim's *already logged-in* accounts is harder.

**Approach:** AmnesiaStealer clones a victim's Chromium browser profile, launches it hidden in headless mode, and connects to it over the browser's own remote-debugging interface, giving the attacker a live video feed and mouse/keyboard control.

**Result:** Full interactive access to 16 Chromium-based browsers' cookies, saved logins, crypto wallets, keychain passwords, and Telegram sessions — while still logged in.

**Why it matters:** First documented macOS malware pairing profile cloning with live remote control, moving past theft into active session hijacking.

**Jargon:** infostealer — malware that harvests saved credentials/data. CDP (Chrome DevTools Protocol) — browser's built-in remote-debugging API. Headless browser — one running with no visible window. ClickFix — fake-verification trick that tricks users into running malicious scripts.

### A strain knob for altermagnets

**Problem:** Altermagnets carry almost no net magnetization yet still break time-reversal symmetry — but natural magnetic domains scramble that signal, making it hard to isolate or control.

**Approach:** Rice University researchers stretched hexagonal manganese telluride along one axis to align its domains, then varied the strain while measuring the anomalous Hall effect, a sideways voltage produced by internal magnetic structure.

**Result:** About 1% strain flipped the Hall signal's polarity entirely without disturbing the magnetic order — an effect otherwise requiring a 150°C temperature swing.

**Why it matters:** A simple mechanical way to control altermagnetic behavior at room temperature, useful for strain-tunable spintronic sensors.

**Jargon:** Altermagnet — magnet with near-zero net moment but broken time-reversal symmetry. Anomalous Hall effect — voltage from internal magnetism, not an external field. Strain — mechanical stretching/deformation. Time-reversal symmetry — physics law that a reversed-time system behaves identically; altermagnets break it.
