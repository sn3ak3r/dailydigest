## Deep digests

### New Research Tests Whether AI Models Know Their Own Thoughts

**Problem:** Can language models notice their own internal states, or do they just echo human talk about minds?
**Approach:** Researchers inject a concept pattern into a model's activity (steering), then ask whether it noticed an injected thought and what it was about; controls get no injection.
**Result:** The strongest Claude flagged and named the concept in ~20% of trials, with no false alarms. Follow-up work suggests naming may come from steering alone; detecting "something is off" held up.
**Why it matters:** Whether anomaly detection counts as introspection is disputed; loose use may make the term meaningless.
**Glossary:** Introspection: reporting on one's own mental states. Steering: amplifying a neural pattern to push outputs toward a concept. Anomaly detection: noticing altered processing without identifying the change. Neural network: interconnected units with trained strengths.
