## Deep Digest: Breaking through AlphaFold's limits to predict how proteins change shape

**Problem:** AlphaFold3 predicts one static protein shape, but many proteins work by switching shapes — missing the biology that matters most.

**Approach:** AF3-ReD reruns AlphaFold3 repeatedly, adding a penalty that pushes each new prediction away from shapes already seen, forcing exploration instead of one fixed answer.

**Result:** It recovered open, closed, and intermediate shapes of ATP synthase's F1β subunit — states plain AlphaFold3 never produced.

**Why it matters:** Drugs bind specific shapes, not an average; modeling a protein's full range makes drug design more realistic.

**Jargon:** Conformation — a protein's 3D shape. Bias energy term — penalty steering predictions away from repeats. ATP synthase — enzyme that makes cellular energy. Diffusion model — AI that builds structures via stepwise denoising.

## Deep Digest: Levitated magnet opens new frontier in search for ultraheavy dark matter

**Problem:** Dark matter could be far heavier than most detectors target, even cell-scale — too massive and rare for standard experiments to catch.

**Approach:** Researchers levitated a milligram magnet above a supercooled superconductor, sensitive to motion a hundredth an atom's width, watching for the "impulse" a heavy particle would leave.

**Result:** A month of data showed no hits, but the null result ruled out nine orders of magnitude of mass combinations, ~10 million times heavier than prior experiments.

**Why it matters:** A null result still narrows where dark matter can hide, reaching a mass range no other experiment covers.

**Jargon:** Superconductor — zero-resistance material near absolute zero. Levitation — suspending an object magnetically. Impulse — a brief force event.
