### Mitotic chromosomes may assemble from pre-existing chromatin 'building blocks' during cell division
[phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-mitotic-chromosomes-pre-chromatin-blocks.html)

Problem: How does a cell compress ~2m of DNA into a tidy mitotic chromosome each division?
Approach: Researchers synthesized decades of chromosome-scaffold and condensin research, modeling chromatin as pre-built blocks, not a blank strand folded from scratch.
Result: Mitotic chromosomes seem to assemble from condensed chromatin domains already present in interphase nuclei, which condensins gather and reorganize into the familiar rod shape.
Why it matters: Treating interphase and mitotic chromatin as one continuous structure reframes how cells ensure accurate chromosome handoff, with implications for genetic instability and disease.
Glossary: Chromatin - DNA packaged with proteins. Condensin - complex that compacts chromosomes. Interphase - part of the cycle between divisions. Mitotic chromosome - coiled structure seen during division.

### Running Linux 6.11 on the ESP32-S3 with a Few Tweaks
[hackaday.com](https://hackaday.com/2026/09/15/running-linux-6-11-on-the-esp32-s3-with-a-few-tweaks/)

Problem: The ESP32-S3 microcontroller has no memory management unit (MMU), hardware Linux normally needs for virtual memory and process isolation.
Approach: Developer Paulneja built a NOMMU Linux config with a custom fork(), running Linux on one CPU core while the other handles WiFi/Bluetooth under FreeRTOS.
Result: Version 0.8 boots reliably with 3.7MB usable RAM on a board with 16MB flash and 8MB octal PSRAM, up from the prior release.
Why it matters: Minimal Linux on a cheap chip opens the door to embedded routers running BusyBox instead of a bespoke RTOS.
Glossary: MMU - manages virtual memory/isolation. NOMMU - Linux variant for chips without one. PSRAM - pseudo-static RAM, denser but slower than SRAM. FreeRTOS - lightweight real-time OS.
