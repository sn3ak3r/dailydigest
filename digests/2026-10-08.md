## Deep digest: As AI Closed In on ‘Unique Games’ Proof, Researchers Raced to Beat the Machines
https://www.quantamagazine.org/as-ai-closed-in-on-unique-games-proof-researchers-raced-to-beat-the-machines-20261007/

**Problem:** Does a long-standing computer-science conjecture (unique games) hold, i.e. is approximating certain constraint puzzles hard? Rumors said an AI had proven it.
**Approach:** Minzer, Fei and Wang found a new error-correcting code and combined it with earlier pieces, racing the rumor to post a 95-page paper.
**Result:** They proved a weaker 4-to-1 version, implying that for 3-colorable graphs no fixed number of extra colors makes coloring easy. OpenAI then announced a Lean-verified AI proof of the 2-to-1 case, not yet expert-reviewed.
**Why it matters:** The conjecture ties together many hardness results; AI-vs-human scooping is now real.
**Glossary:** Constraint satisfaction (meet as many rules as possible); Graph coloring (color nodes so edges obey rules); Error-correcting code (redundant encoding); Lean (formal proof checker).
