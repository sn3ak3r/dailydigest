## Deep Digests

### Gravity Seems Holographic. What Does That Mean for Reality?
**Problem:** Can everything happening inside a region of space be fully described by information living only on its surface, not its volume?
**Approach:** Bekenstein and Hawking found black hole entropy scales with surface area, not volume, hinting the interior is redundant. Late-1990s AdS/CFT proofs then showed a gravity-filled interior space is mathematically identical to a gravity-free quantum theory on its boundary.
**Result:** Gravity and quantum mechanics turn out to be two descriptions of the same system, one 3D-with-gravity, one 2D-without.
**Why it matters:** If this holds generally, our 3D reality may be a projection of 2D quantum information — a leading path to unifying gravity with quantum mechanics.
**Jargon:** Holographic principle — a volume's information fits on its boundary. AdS/CFT — the gravity/boundary-theory equivalence. Anti-de Sitter space — a curved space where infinite volume fits a finite boundary. Entanglement entropy — quantum correlation between inside and outside.
(link: https://www.quantamagazine.org/gravity-seems-holographic-what-does-that-mean-for-reality-20260925/)

### ShinyHunters hacked Clop's leak site using a Grav CMS path traversal flaw
**Problem:** Grav CMS's 1.7 branch let unauthenticated users upload files outside the intended directory.
**Approach:** Attackers put directory-traversal sequences like `../../../shhq` into the `__unique_form_id__` form parameter, so Grav wrote uploads outside the protected `tmp/forms` folder — no login needed.
**Result:** ShinyHunters breached Clop's own ransomware leak site, claimed to steal source, plugins, logs and private keys, then defaced it and demanded ransom; Clop relocated to a new Tor address.
**Why it matters:** Grav 2.0 patched this in early 2026, but the still-common 1.7 branch didn't — even ransomware crews run unpatched legacy software.
**Jargon:** Path traversal — using `../` sequences to escape an intended directory. CVE — the public identifier for a disclosed flaw (CVE-2026-42608 here). Sanitization — filtering input to strip dangerous sequences.
(link: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/shinyhunters-hacked-clop-leak-site-using-grav-cms-path-traversal-flaw/)
