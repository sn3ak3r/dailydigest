## Deep Digest: Entangled Particles Revive a 35-Year-Old Test of the Standard Model

**Problem:** A key Standard Model number, VUS (the probability a strange quark converts to an up quark), has disagreed between measurement methods, and physicists needed an independent check.

**Approach:** The BESIII Collaboration collided electrons and positrons to create quantum-entangled pairs of lambda hyperons, then used one particle's decay to reconstruct otherwise-invisible details of its partner — a technique unused for 35 years — across roughly 10 billion collision events.

**Result:** The measured VUS value is consistent with a balanced CKM matrix, backing the Standard Model, but stays in mild tension with older kaon-based measurements.

**Why it matters:** An independent cross-check keeps alive the possibility that the discrepancy signals physics beyond the Standard Model.

**Glossary:** CKM matrix (quark-transformation probability table), quantum entanglement (linked particles sharing correlated states), hyperon (heavy unstable particle), Standard Model (particle physics' current theory).

## Deep Digest: A Vacuum Tube Computer For The Home

**Problem:** Historic vacuum-tube computers like ENIAC needed room-sized hardware and wartime budgets, making the technology look impractical for hobbyists.

**Approach:** Builder Mike designed an 8-bit von Neumann machine with 16 instructions, using recycled Eastern Bloc double-triode tubes and a NOR-based logic scheme borrowed from NASA's Apollo-era computers, plus modern parts for I/O.

**Result:** The finished machine is a compact, wall-mounted unit driving a flip-digit display and a simple airship simulator game.

**Why it matters:** It shows vacuum-tube computing is achievable on a hobbyist budget, keeping a nearly extinct technology approachable for retrocomputing fans.

**Glossary:** Von Neumann architecture (shared memory/processor design), double-triode (dual-amplifier vacuum tube), NOR-based design (logic built from NOR gates), retrocomputing (recreating vintage computer technology).
