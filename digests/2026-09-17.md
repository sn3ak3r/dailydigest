## Deep Digests

### A 3D Rasterizer For Embedded Devices
[hackaday.com](https://hackaday.com/2026/09/16/a-3d-rasterizer-for-embedded-devices/)

Problem: Rendering real 3D graphics on a microcontroller is hard without dedicated GPU hardware or much memory.
Approach: Jet is a dependency-free, integer-only C++17 software rasterizer built for chips like the ESP32 and STM32, targeting RGB565 SPI displays.
Result: On an ESP32-S3 it renders about 650 post-culled triangles at 60fps (1300 at 30fps) on a 480×320 display — performance the developers place between the Sega 32X and Saturn.
Why it matters: It brings real-time 3D to sub-$20 microcontrollers with no GPU, opening the door to retro-style 3D on badges and embedded builds.
Glossary: Rasterizer - converts 3D triangles into pixels. RGB565 - 16-bit packed color format. SPI - serial bus used to drive small displays. Post-culling - triangles left after removing hidden/off-screen ones.

### Mobile trap transports 92 antiprotons by road and stores them for over a month in world first
[phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-mobile-antiprotons-road-month-world.html)

Problem: Precisely comparing protons and antiprotons, to hunt for matter-antimatter asymmetry, is limited at CERN by magnetic-field noise from facility operations.
Approach: The BASE collaboration loaded 92 antiprotons into BASE-STEP, a mobile Penning trap, then drove it about 8km around CERN by truck.
Result: The antiprotons survived transport and were stored for over a month, with vacuum better than 2.2×10⁻¹⁸ mbar — the first road transport and longest open-trap antimatter storage on record.
Why it matters: Portable antimatter storage lets physicists take precision measurements to quieter labs, chasing a matter-antimatter difference that could explain why the universe has any matter left at all.
Glossary: Penning trap - electromagnetic device that confines charged particles. ppb - parts per billion, a precision unit. Matter-antimatter asymmetry - unexplained excess of matter over antimatter in the cosmos. mbar - millibar, a unit of vacuum pressure.
