## Deep digests

### Appraisal by AI: Model-driven victim prioritization in the VOLTA infostealer
**Problem:** Infostealers dump huge piles of credentials; deciding which victims are worth attacking is slow manual work.

**Approach:** VOLTA, a self-deleting Windows stealer, builds a profile of each victim (employer, location, cards, wallets), enriches it with stolen sessions, then sends it via OpenRouter to an LLM prompted as a "forensic intelligence analyst." If the default model (Claude Opus 4.6) refuses, it falls back to Kimi K2, then Gemini 2.5 Flash.

**Result:** A ranked list of valuable assets per victim, exfiltrated in up to three staged archives. Config obfuscation is weak and recoverable by reverse engineering.

**Why it matters:** Cheap builder-model malware can automate victim triage, and refusal-fallback chains may become standard.

**Glossary:** Infostealer: malware harvesting credentials/cookies. Session token: login credential bypassing passwords. Fallback chain: backup models tried on refusal. Builder model: author compiles custom binaries for customers.
