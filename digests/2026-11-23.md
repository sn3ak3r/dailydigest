### Building a Single-Chip USB GPU

**Problem:** Can you make a working video adapter from nearly nothing?

**Approach:** bitluni built a USB-to-analog-video converter on one tiny PCB around a ~20-cent CH570 microcontroller. The USB-C plug and RCA plug are both shaped from the board itself. A crystal keeps timing locked to the TV's refresh; hot code runs from SRAM because flash was too slow; PWM synthesizes the analog signal.

**Result:** It enumerates as a USB serial device and shows greyscale text and simple graphics — usable as a serial terminal. A TV even picked up the PWM leaking from a jumper wire, hinting at PAL colour via overclocked SPI.

**Why it matters:** Shows how far tight timing and clever PCB design stretch a bare MCU.

**Glossary:**
- *CDC* — USB serial class
- *PWM* — rapid on/off switching averaged into a voltage
- *SRAM* — fast on-chip RAM
- *PAL* — analog colour TV standard.
