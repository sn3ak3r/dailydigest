### Central Surface Density of Dark Matter Agrees With the Prediction of a New Theory of Gravity
[phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-central-surface-density-dark-theory.html)

Problem: Galaxies of wildly different brightness all show a puzzlingly similar "central surface density" of dark matter, a regularity plain dark-matter models don't explain.
Approach: A researcher applied Erik Verlinde's "emergent gravity," a dark-matter-free alternative, after fixing a math limitation in the 2016 original that broke when combining gravity from multiple masses.
Result: The corrected calculation predicts about 170 solar masses per square parsec, landing right inside the 90-220 range measured across real galaxies.
Why it matters: It doesn't rule out dark matter, but shows a gravity-only theory can reproduce this puzzle, motivating harder tests of modified gravity in weak-gravity regimes.
Glossary: Emergent gravity - gravity arising from a deeper thermodynamic process rather than being fundamental. Surface density - mass packed into a patch of sky. Solar mass - the Sun's mass. Parsec - about 3.26 light-years.

### A JavaScript OS for the ESP32
[hackaday.com](https://hackaday.com/2026/09/23/a-javascript-os-for-the-esp32/)

Problem: Firmware for ESP32 microcontrollers is normally written in C/C++, a barrier for the much larger pool of developers who only know JavaScript.
Approach: KryonOS packages a JavaScript interpreter, a simple GUI/graphics library, SD-card filesystem access, and full hardware APIs into a thin OS layer that boots on cheap ESP32 display boards, plus a built-in app store.
Result: It already runs on widely available boards like the "Cheap Yellow Display," letting users write working GUI apps in JavaScript instead of C.
Why it matters: It follows the path MicroPython carved for Python, lowering the barrier to hobbyist and prototype hardware projects.
Glossary: ESP32 - a cheap Wi-Fi/Bluetooth microcontroller chip. Interpreter - software that runs code directly instead of compiling it. Firmware - software built into a hardware device. GUI - graphical user interface.
