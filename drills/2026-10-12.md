# Decode-one-instruction drill — 2026-10-12

Target: Windows x86-64.

Decode this instruction:

```
C1 E2 05
```

What is the assembly? Which register is modified, and what is the arithmetic effect?

---

## Answer

`shl edx, 5` (edx = edx × 32)

- `C1` = shift/rotate group, `r/m32, imm8`; the ModRM byte `E2` = `11 100 010`: mod=11 (register-direct), reg=100 (/4 = SHL, an opcode extension, not a register), rm=010 (edx).
- The last byte `05` is the imm8 shift count, so the shift is left by 5 bits, i.e. multiply by 2^5 = 32.
- No REX prefix means 32-bit operand size; writing edx zero-extends into rdx's upper half.
