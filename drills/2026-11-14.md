# Crackme: find the input (Windows x64, arg in ecx)

```
check:
    mov    eax, ecx
    xor    eax, 0x5A
    rol    eax, 4
    cmp    eax, 0x50000006
    jne    fail
    mov    eax, 1
    ret
fail:
    xor    eax, eax
    ret
```

`rol eax, 4` rotates left by 4 bits: bits shifted out of the top re-enter at the bottom (unlike `shl`, nothing is lost). Which unsigned `x` (ecx) makes `check` return 1?

---

**Answer:** `x = 0x6500005A` (1694498906)

1. Work backwards: undo `rol 4` with `ror 4` (rotate right). 0x50000006 → the low nibble `6` wraps to the top, giving 0x65000000.
2. `xor` is its own inverse: 0x65000000 ^ 0x5A = 0x6500005A.
3. Check forwards: 0x6500005A ^ 0x5A = 0x65000000; rotating left 4 moves the top `6` round to the bottom → 0x50000006. A `shl` would have dropped that `6`, so the wrap is the whole point.
