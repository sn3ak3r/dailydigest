# Crackme: find the input (Windows x64, arg in ecx)

```
check:
    movzx  eax, cx          ; 0F B7: low 16 bits, zero-extended
    shr    ecx, 16          ; high 16 bits slide down
    cmp    ecx, 0xC0DE
    jne    fail
    add    eax, ecx
    cmp    eax, 0x11111
    jne    fail
    mov    eax, 1
    ret
fail:
    xor    eax, eax
    ret
```

`cx` is the low 16 bits (a "word") of `ecx`. `movzx eax, cx` copies that word into `eax` and fills the upper 16 bits with zeros. Which unsigned `x` (ecx) makes `check` return 1?

---

**Answer:** `x = 0xC0DE5033` (3235794995)

1. The function splits `x` into two words: `eax` = low half, `ecx` = high half (`shr 16` discards the low bits). The first `cmp` pins the high half to 0xC0DE.
2. Then low + 0xC0DE must equal 0x11111, so low = 0x11111 − 0xC0DE = 0x5033. The sum is 32-bit, so nothing wraps.
3. Glue the halves back together: high word 0xC0DE, low word 0x5033 → 0xC0DE5033.
