# Mini Crackme — 2026-08-17

Disassembly of `check` (Windows x64, single argument passed in `ecx`):

```asm
check:
    mov     eax, ecx
    shr     eax, 4
    and     eax, 0Fh
    cmp     eax, 5
    jne     fail
    mov     eax, ecx
    and     eax, 0Fh
    cmp     eax, 0Ah
    jne     fail
    mov     eax, 1
    ret
fail:
    mov     eax, 0
    ret
```

Find the decimal value (0–255) passed in `ecx` that makes `check` return 1.

---

**Answer: 90 (0x5A)**

- The first block shifts the input right 4 bits and masks with `0Fh`, isolating the high nibble — it must equal `5`.
- The second block masks the original value with `0Fh` directly, isolating the low nibble — it must equal `0xA`.
- The only byte satisfying both nibble constraints is `0x5A`, which is 90 in decimal.
