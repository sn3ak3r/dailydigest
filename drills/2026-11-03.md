# Decode-one-instruction drill — 2026-11-03

Target: Windows x86-64.

Decode this instruction:

```
0F 4F C2
```

What is the assembly, and what does it do? Where does the result go, and what does it replace branching for?

---

## Answer

`cmovg eax, edx`

- `0F 4x` is the CMOVcc family; the low nibble is the condition code, `F` = G (greater, signed: ZF=0 and SF=OF). ModRM `C2` = `11 000 010`: mod=11 register-direct, reg=000 (eax, destination), rm=010 (edx, source).
- If the condition holds, `eax = edx`; otherwise eax is unchanged. No jump, no flags modified.
- After `cmp eax, edx` this is `if (edx > eax) eax = edx;` i.e. a branchless signed max.
