# 2026-09-06 — Decode One Instruction (Windows x86-64)

Decode this instruction (Intel syntax), byte by byte:

```
83 7C C8 10 05
```

Give the mnemonic and full operands.

---

**Answer:** `cmp dword ptr [rax+rcx*8+0x10], 0x5`

**Explanation:**
- `83` is the Group-1 opcode for an r/m32 destination with a **sign-extended imm8** source; the `/digit` in ModRM's reg field picks the specific op (here `/7` = CMP, not ADD/SUB/etc).
- ModRM `7C` = `01 111 100`: mod=01 (disp8 follows), reg=111 (selects CMP), rm=100 (means "read a SIB byte" instead of naming a register directly).
- SIB `C8` = `11 001 000`: scale=11₂ (×8), index=001 (RCX), base=000 (RAX) → address = `rax + rcx*8`. The trailing bytes are disp8=`0x10` and imm8=`0x05`, giving the final `[rax+rcx*8+0x10], 0x5` comparison.
